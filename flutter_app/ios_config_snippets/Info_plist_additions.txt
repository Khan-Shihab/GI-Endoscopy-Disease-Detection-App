Add these keys inside ios/Runner/Info.plist, as siblings of the other
<key>...</key><string>...</string> pairs:

<key>NSCameraUsageDescription</key>
<string>This app needs camera access to capture endoscopy images for polyp detection.</string>

<key>NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription</key>
<string>This app needs photo library access to select endoscopy images for polyp detection.</string>

Without these, iOS will silently kill the app the moment image_picker
tries to request camera or photo library access.
